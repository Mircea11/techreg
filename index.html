Mission

TechReg Observatory aims to reduce the burden of regulatory monitoring for European organisations by translating AI, data and cybersecurity regulation into clear guidance, obligation maps, controls, dashboards and source-based assistance.

The project is designed to help AI developers and companies move faster while addressing legal concerns, public trust, compliance requirements and responsible innovation.

Who it is for

AI developers

Fast orientation on applicable obligations, documentation and risk controls.

Companies

Practical implementation support for SMEs, industrial firms and larger organisations.

Students

Beginner-friendly explanations for law, regulatory, IT, cybersecurity and AI students.

Specialists

Structured material for compliance, data, legal, cybersecurity and industry professionals.

Initial regulatory scope

  • EU AI Act
  • GDPR
  • NIS2 Directive
  • Cyber Resilience Act
  • Data Act
  • Machinery Regulation
  • Industrial AI and cybersecurity standards

Planned modules

Document register

A controlled list of official regulatory sources and guidance documents.

Obligation matrix

Regulation mapped to business processes, data needs, controls and evidence.

Concept explainer

Plain-language explanations from beginner to expert level.

RAG assistant

A future source-based assistant answering only from the approved document corpus.

Status

Early public prototype. The current phase focuses on the website structure, regulatory document register and proof-of-work repository.
