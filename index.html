Mission

TechReg Observatory aims to reduce the burden of regulatory monitoring for European organisations by translating AI, data and cybersecurity regulation into clear guidance, obligation maps, controls, dashboards and source-based assistance.

The objective is not to add another layer of bureaucracy. The objective is to help European AI developers and companies move faster with clarity, while addressing legal concerns, public trust and responsible innovation.

Core idea

Regulation should become an operating system for trust, quality and competitiveness, not only an administrative burden.

Who it is for

TechReg is designed for both practical implementation and education. It should help users move from basic understanding to professional application.

AI developers

Fast orientation on applicable rules, documentation needs and risk controls.

Companies

Support for SMEs, industrial firms and larger organisations implementing EU rules.

Students

Structured entry point for law, regulatory, IT, cybersecurity, data and AI students.

Specialists

Material for compliance, legal, data, cybersecurity and industrial professionals.

Initial regulatory scope

TechReg starts with the core EU digital regulation stack affecting AI, data, cybersecurity and industrial organisations.

Instrument Main focus Why it matters
EU AI Act AI classification, high-risk systems, documentation, transparency and oversight Core framework for AI governance and implementation readiness.
GDPR Personal data, lawful basis, DPIA, rights and automated processing Applies whenever AI or digital systems process personal data.
NIS2 Directive Cybersecurity risk management, incident reporting and supply-chain security Important for energy, industrial and other critical sectors.
Cyber Resilience Act Secure digital products, vulnerabilities, updates and conformity Relevant for software, connected products and industrial digital tools.
Data Act Connected-product data, IoT data access, data sharing and portability Important for industrial data, smart devices and predictive maintenance.
Machinery Regulation Safety, software in machinery, AI-enabled systems and conformity assessment Relevant when AI or software enters machinery and industrial equipment.

How it works

TechReg is designed as a structured workflow from official regulatory sources to practical implementation support.

1

Register documents

Collect official EU, national authority and guidance documents in a controlled source register.

2

Extract obligations

Translate legal and technical requirements into obligations, functions and business processes.

3

Assess organisations

Match obligations with company profile, sector, AI use cases and cybersecurity exposure.

4

Map controls

Define controls, evidence requirements, owners and management KPIs.

5

Support decisions

Use dashboards and a future source-based RAG assistant to support implementation.
