TechReg Observatory

AI, Data & Cybersecurity Regulation Observatory

Mission

TechReg helps European AI developers, organisations, students and professionals understand and implement EU AI, data and cybersecurity regulation.

The project translates complex legal requirements into practical guidance, obligation maps, controls, dashboards and source-based AI assistance.

Initial Regulatory Scope

  • EU AI Act
  • GDPR
  • NIS2 Directive
  • Cyber Resilience Act
  • Data Act
  • Machinery Regulation

Planned Modules

  • Document register
  • Obligation matrix
  • Concept glossary
  • Company assessment
  • Readiness dashboard
  • Source-based RAG assistant
